# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 5, 2026

## Privacy Policy

Thank you for using our Discord bot.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information that may be collected while using the bot.

### Information We Collect

Our bot may collect and process the following information:

- Discord User IDs
- Discord Server IDs
- Channel IDs
- Message IDs (only when necessary for bot functionality)

The bot does **not** collect passwords, payment information, or any sensitive personal information.

### How We Use Information

The information collected is used only to:

- Provide the bot's features and commands.
- Improve functionality and performance.
- Prevent abuse and ensure security.

### Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties unless required by law.

### Data Storage

Data is stored only as long as necessary for the bot to function correctly. Unnecessary data is periodically removed.

### Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access.

### Your Rights

You may request the deletion of any data associated with your Discord account by contacting the bot developer.

### Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the bot indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

### Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the bot developer through Discord.
